Punisher

Punisher is a great example of saying something devastating as if it’s a joke. Bridgers writes about depression, broken relationships, and her own dread with dark, specific humor, so the saddest lines often land as punchlines first. The title track is a tender, slightly embarrassed song about her hero Elliott Smith. “Kyoto” shows she can write a bright, horn-backed indie-rock song that still hurts. The record ends with “I Know the End,” which grows from a quiet road-trip song into a wall of horns, drums, and screaming. It came out in mid-2020, and that ending felt exactly right for the moment.
