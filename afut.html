Ants From Up There

By Black Country New Road, This is a big, emotional chamber-rock record, and the playing makes it work. Sax, violin, and piano wrap around Isaac Wood’s shaky, desperate vocals, and the songs build slowly until they finally break open. The Concorde comes back again and again as a picture of something you can’t hold onto, which gives the album a loose story about wanting and losing someone. It all leads to “Basketball Shoes,” a nearly 13-minute closer that may be the most cathartic ending of the decade so far. Wood left the band days before the album came out, so it also sounds like the end of something, and that makes it hit even harder.
